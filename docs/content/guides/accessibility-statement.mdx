Public-sector sites in Sweden must publish an accessibility statement (DOS-lagen), and the European Accessibility Act applies to many private services since June 2025. grounded-ui gives you text you can reuse.

## What each component page gives you

- **WCAG** — the success criteria the component meets and how. Written to be pasted into a statement.
- **What the site must handle** — the criteria that depend on your site, not the component. Contrast, focus order, page language and similar.

## How to use it

1. List the components your site uses.
2. Copy each **WCAG** table into your statement's description of the component.
3. Check every **What the site must handle** item on your own pages. These are where statements usually go wrong.
4. Test with real content. The contract guarantees the structure is right; it can't guarantee that an error message is understandable.

## The honest limit

A component can meet its contract while the page around it fails: low contrast, a broken heading order, a form without an error summary. grounded-ui guarantees the component's contract and documents the boundary. The rest is the site's.
